The module

Everything connected to one employee record.

The Staff Profile & Details module is the master data foundation. Every other process reads from it.

Main focus

Staff Profile & Details

Seven clean tabs covering personal, employment, family, statutory, documents and activity — with field-level masking and an audited edit flow.

Attendance & clock history

Staff clock in and out. The Boss gets a read-only team attendance view for performance monitoring — no personal clock-in.

Leave & shared calendar

Balances, applications and approvals. Approved leave lands on the calendar instantly; HR and Boss can drag items to a corrected date.

Claims

Submit and track expense claims. The owner sees a read-only overview with no New Claim button.

Staff Directory

HR and Boss open a separate staff profile page from any row — never their own profile by mistake.

Comfortable by default

Collapsible sidebar, minimised brand state and a dark-mode switch — royal blue and silver white throughout.
